Issue certificates

Fill in the details and you get a certificate ID, a public link and a QR code. Then upload the small record file to the data folder on GitHub. That upload is what makes the certificate verifiable.

You opened this page from a folder on your computer. That works for creating IDs and QR codes, but first paste your GitHub Pages address under “Site address” below, so the links and QR codes point to the right place.
Site address:

This is where your public page lives. Every certificate link is this address plus the ID. It is detected automatically when you open this page from GitHub Pages, so you normally never touch it.

Only the name column is required. Other columns you may add: organization, level, description, period, program, title, date, category, theme. Anything you leave out is taken from the boxes below, so fill those with what the whole batch has in common. A plain list of names, one per line, also works.

Everything typed here becomes public on the verification page. Do not enter email addresses, phone numbers or private notes.

Change or revoke an existing certificate

Load the record, change what is needed (or set the status to Revoked), then download the file again and upload it to the data folder, replacing the old one. The ID, link and QR code stay the same.
